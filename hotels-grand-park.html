Where to Stay Near Grand Park (Westfield, Indiana)

Grand Park Sports Campus — 711 E 191st St, Westfield, IN 46074 — has 26 baseball and softball diamonds, and its tournaments pull families in from all over Indiana and beyond. Most families book 2–3 nights for a tournament weekend, and the hotels closest to the fields sell out first on the big weekends. The six hotels below are all within about 13 minutes of the park, with drive times and typical summer-weekend prices. Wyndham Westfield — closest to the fields

18592 Carousel Ln, Westfield, IN 46074 · 2 min / 0.4 mi to Grand Park

The only hotel you can walk to the park from — Grand Park is across the street. 152 rooms and suites, heated indoor pool, arcade and game room for the kids between games, and the Winners Circle restaurant and bar on site, so dinner is sorted without driving anywhere. This is the hotel that sells out first on big tournament weekends, so it is the one to book earliest.

Typical summer weekend: ~$250–$300 / night. See it on Expedia →

Note: this hotel used to be the Cambria Hotel Westfield — same building at the same address, new name.

Holiday Inn Express & Suites Westfield — newest build

18975 Sunny Dr, Westfield, IN 46074 · 4 min / 1.3 mi to Grand Park

The newest hotel on this list — built in 2025, 97 rooms, all with microwaves and 55-inch smart TVs. Free Express Start hot breakfast, heated indoor pool, free parking, and a 13-minute walk to the fields if you would rather stretch your legs than drive. Rated 9.4 out of 10 on Expedia from 190 reviews.

Typical summer weekend: ~$200–$280 / night. See it on Expedia →

SpringHill Suites Westfield — best for a family of four

19317 Westmore Ln, Westfield, IN 46074 · 4 min / 1.3 mi to Grand Park

Every room is a suite: separate sitting area, sofa bed, mini-fridge and microwave, so two kids, two parents, and a pile of gear actually fit. Free hot breakfast, indoor pool, 24-hour fitness center, and the Oak Bar on site. Suites that sleep six (two queens plus a sofa bed) are the ones to ask for if you are bringing a sibling along.

Typical summer weekend: ~$250–$330 / night. See it on Expedia →

Hampton Inn Westfield — free breakfast, pet-friendly

17400 Wheeler Rd, Westfield, IN 46074 · 5 min / 1.6 mi to Grand Park

The hotel that calls itself the closest to Grand Park, and it is nearly that — about two miles by the road. Free hot breakfast, indoor pool, fitness center, free parking, and EV charging. The Monon Trail runs right past the hotel, so a morning run or a walk with the dog is easy. Pets are welcome for a $75 fee per stay.

Typical summer weekend: ~$250–$300 / night. See it on Expedia →

Holiday Inn Express Carmel North — lowest summer-weekend price

15131 Thatcher Ln, Carmel, IN 46033 · 9 min / 4.2 mi to Grand Park

The cheapest of the six on a summer weekend, and the most practical for a whole team: free hot breakfast buffet, heated indoor pool, free parking with room for buses and trailers, and dogs allowed. It is in Carmel rather than Westfield, so the drive is nine minutes — still shorter than the gap between most Grand Park fields and the parking lot on a busy Saturday.

Typical summer weekend: ~$200–$270 / night. See it on Expedia →

Home2 Suites Carmel — in-room kitchen

12845 Old Meridian St, Carmel, IN 46032 · 13 min / 6.9 mi to Grand Park

Every room is a suite with a full kitchen — fridge, dishwasher, microwave, cookware — so a family staying Friday through Sunday can cook breakfast and pack lunches instead of eating out three times a day. The trade-off is the drive: 13 minutes, and you are in Carmel's Arts & Design District, which has the best restaurants near the park.

Booking tips for tournament weekends

Book a refundable rate while the schedule is not final. Game times and brackets shift, and a team can be done a day early. A refundable rate costs a little more up front, but it beats paying for a night nobody sleeps in.

Check your tournament's hotel block first. Many Grand Park tournaments run a hotel block, and some require teams to book through it. The block rate is often the best one on offer — and booking outside it can mean your team does not count toward the room-night requirement.

The four Westfield hotels go first. Wyndham, Holiday Inn Express Westfield, SpringHill, and Hampton are all within two miles of the fields. If they are full, Carmel — 9 to 13 minutes south — has plenty of rooms and better restaurants, and the drive is an easy one.

Keep the team together. If several families book the same hotel, ask the front desk about connecting rooms or a group rate. The Wyndham and SpringHill both have meeting space that works for a team dinner between games.

Plan meals around the park's rules. Grand Park does not allow spectator coolers or outside food and drinks, so the hotel breakfast matters — every hotel on this page includes one — and the restaurants around Westfield and Carmel are where teams eat between games.
