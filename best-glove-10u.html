Best Baseball Glove for 10U: 11″ to 11.5″, Picks by Position

A 10U player needs an 11 to 11.5 inch glove — 11 inches for smaller 9-year-olds and infield-first kids, 11.5 inches for a typical 10-year-old and outfield innings. 11.75 inches is the ceiling. At 10U, hands are finally strong enough for a properly shaped pocket, real ground balls come off real bats, and a soft tee-ball glove is now actively costing outs. Full glove guide by age & position →

Before you order — the throwing-hand check: a Right Hand Throw (RHT) glove is worn on the left hand. It's still the most common ordering mistake at every age. Confirm which hand your player throws with, then order that throw.

Does position matter yet at 10U?

A little. A committed infielder can go 11 inches with a shallow pocket and an open web (I-web or H-web) for quick transfers. An outfield-leaning kid goes 11.5 inches with a deeper pocket. Pitchers want a closed web to hide their grip. But most 10U players still rotate — if yours does, buy the 11.5″ utility glove above and stop there. Catchers need a real mitt (about 31″ around at this age), and first basemen need a mitt, not a glove — both are on the full glove guide.

The 10-second fit test

Put the glove on, ball in the pocket, squeeze. If your player can close it on their own in under 10 seconds without using both hands, it fits. If they can't, it's too stiff or too big — size down or pick an easy-close model like the Sure Catch above. A glove that can't be closed is a glove that drops balls.
