Where to Stay in Indianapolis for Baseball Families

Indianapolis is the center of tournament baseball in the state — Victory Field (501 W Maryland St), home of the Indianapolis Indians, anchors downtown, and Perfect Game’s Pastime Butler University/UIndy events — the Midwest Tournament of Champions (June 11–14), the All Scout World Series (July 2–5), and the Great Lakes Select Showcase (July 9–12) — play games at Butler University, the University of Indianapolis, and high-school fields around the city. Most families book 2–3 nights for a tournament weekend, and the hotels closest to the fields sell out first on the big weekends. The six hotels below are five downtown hotels within about 5 minutes of Victory Field, plus one on the north side by Butler University, with drive times and typical summer-weekend prices. JW Marriott Indianapolis — closest to the ballpark

10 S West St, Indianapolis, IN 46204 · 3 min / 0.2 mi to Victory Field (4-min walk)

The closest hotel on this page to the ballpark — Victory Field is across the street, about a four-minute walk, and the field is visible from the upper floors. 1,005 rooms in a 33-story glass tower, an indoor pool and whirlpool, a 24-hour fitness center, and the High Velocity sports bar for after the game, plus Dean’s Steak & Seafood and a Starbucks on site. One honest trade-off: it is the priciest hotel on this page, and breakfast costs extra — but for a family that wants to walk to the game and never move the car, nothing else is this close.

Typical summer weekend: ~$280–$400 / night. See it on Expedia →

Note: rated 9.4 out of 10 on Expedia. Valet parking costs extra.

Fairfield Inn & Suites Indianapolis Downtown — lowest summer-weekend price downtown

501 W Washington St, Indianapolis, IN 46204 · 3 min / 0.3 mi to Victory Field (6-min walk)

The cheapest of the five downtown hotels on a summer weekend, and the simplest value on the list: free hot breakfast every morning, 168 rooms including 34 suites with separate living areas, free Wi-Fi, and a 24-hour fitness center. It sits across from White River State Park, with a skywalk to the Convention Center and Lucas Oil Stadium and the Canal Walk right outside the door for a between-games stroll. One honest trade-off: there is no pool here, and pets are not allowed — but the rooms sleep a family comfortably and breakfast is handled before an early game. Self-parking is $40 a day.

Typical summer weekend: ~$150–$220 / night. See it on Expedia →

Crowne Plaza Indianapolis Downtown Union Station — sleep in a train car

123 W Louisiana St, Indianapolis, IN 46225 · 4 min / 0.5 mi to Victory Field (11-min walk)

The one the kids will remember: a hotel inside America’s first Union Station, with 26 of its 273 rooms set in authentic early-1900s Pullman train cars on their original tracks. Two restaurants on site — 123 West for breakfast and Taggart’s, a full-service sports bar, for dinner — plus a 24-hour fitness center and a skywalk to the Convention Center. One honest trade-off: parking is valet-only at $65 a night, the priciest on this page, and breakfast costs extra. Ask for a train-car room early — there are only 26 of them.

Typical summer weekend: ~$180–$260 / night. See it on Expedia →

Embassy Suites by Hilton Indianapolis Downtown — all suites, free breakfast

110 W Washington St, Indianapolis, IN 46204 · 4 min / 0.6 mi to Victory Field (12-min walk)

Every room is a two-room suite — a separate bedroom and living room with a sofa bed, mini-fridge, and microwave — so two kids, two parents, and a pile of gear actually fit. Free cooked-to-order breakfast every morning and a free evening reception every day, an indoor pool and hot tub, and the E’terie Bar & Grill on site. It is connected by skywalk to the Convention Center and Circle Centre Mall. One honest trade-off: self-parking is $48 a day, and the entrance is up a parking-garage ramp rather than off the street. Pet-friendly ($75 for stays up to 4 nights).

Typical summer weekend: ~$230–$340 / night. See it on Expedia →

Note: the indoor pool is closed for renovation through Jan 31, 2027, and reopens for the summer season.

Hilton Indianapolis Hotel & Suites — indoor pool under a sky dome

120 W Market St, Indianapolis, IN 46204 · 5 min / 0.7 mi to Victory Field (14-min walk)

352 rooms between Monument Circle and the State Capitol, with the best pool setup on this page — a heated indoor pool under a bright sky dome — plus a 24-hour fitness center with Peloton bikes. Two restaurants on site (120 West Bar & Grill and McCormick & Schmick’s), room service, and a Topgolf Swing Suite the kids can book between games. Breakfast costs extra, and parking in the attached covered garage costs extra too, but the pool and the location make it the best all-rounder downtown for a family that wants to swim.

Typical summer weekend: ~$200–$300 / night. See it on Expedia →

Ironworks Hotel Indy — closest to Butler University

2721 E 86th St, Indianapolis, IN 46240 · 21 min / 6.4 mi to Butler University (Expedia); ~20 min to Victory Field

The pick when your games are at Butler — 21 minutes from campus, and the only hotel on this page on the north side, by the Fashion Mall at Keystone. A 120-room industrial-chic boutique hotel with a Michelin Key and a AAA Four Diamond rating: four restaurants on site (Provision, Rize, Blue Sushi Sake Grill, and Daredevil Hall), three bars, a 24-hour fitness center, and free self-parking — the only free parking on this page. Pet-friendly (about $30 a pet a night), and the Heritage rooms with two queens sleep six. The trade-off is the drive downtown: about 20 minutes to Victory Field, so this is the Butler-weekend hotel, not the Indians-weekend one.

Booking tips for tournament weekends

Book a refundable rate while the schedule is not final. Game times and brackets shift, and a team can be done a day early. A refundable rate costs a little more up front, but it beats paying for a night nobody sleeps in.

Check your tournament’s hotel block first. Many Indianapolis tournaments run a hotel block, and some require teams to book through it. The block rate is often the best one on offer — and booking outside it can mean your team does not count toward the room-night requirement.

The five downtown hotels go first. JW Marriott, Fairfield, Crowne Plaza, Embassy Suites, and Hilton are all within a 5-minute drive — or a 15-minute walk — of Victory Field. If they are full, Ironworks by Butler is about 20 minutes north with free parking and four restaurants on site.

Mind the parking. Downtown parking runs $40 a day for self-parking at the Fairfield, $48 at the Embassy Suites, and $65 for valet-only at the Crowne Plaza, and valet costs extra at the JW Marriott and the Hilton too. Ironworks includes self-parking free. Add it to the weekend math before you compare nightly prices.

Plan for games in more than one place. Perfect Game’s Butler/UIndy events spread games across Butler University, the University of Indianapolis, and high-school fields around the city, so check your team’s schedule before picking a hotel. Downtown to Butler is about 12–15 minutes by car, and downtown to UIndy about 10 minutes.

Watch out for the big downtown weekends. When the Colts play at Lucas Oil Stadium, or a convention fills the center, downtown rates can double or triple and the best rooms sell out months ahead. The Indy 500 in May and Gen Con in August push rates up across the city too. Summer tournament weekends are calmer, but book as soon as the tournament dates are set.

Keep the team together. If several families book the same hotel, ask the front desk about connecting rooms or a group rate. The JW Marriott and the Embassy Suites both have meeting space that works for a team dinner between games.
