Where to Stay Near Fort Wayne Tournaments

Fort Wayne’s youth tournaments center on downtown — Parkview Field (1301 Ewing St), home of the TinCaps, and the World Baseball Academy’s fields at 1701 Freeman St, just south of downtown, where the WBA Summer Hoosier Classic (June 3–6, 2027, 10U–15U) and a full summer of Hoosier Classic events are played. Most families book 2–3 nights for a tournament weekend, and the hotels closest to the fields sell out first on the big weekends. The six hotels below are all within about 12 minutes of Parkview Field, with drive times and typical summer-weekend prices. Hampton Inn & Suites Fort Wayne Downtown — closest to the fields

223 W Jefferson Blvd, Fort Wayne, IN 46802 · 3 min / 0.2 mi to Parkview Field (3-min walk)

The closest hotel on this list to the ballpark — Parkview Field is a three-minute walk, and the rooftop bar, Conner’s Rooftop, looks right over the TinCaps’ outfield. Free hot breakfast every morning, the walk-up Burger Bar for lunch and dinner, pet-friendly rooms ($50 per stay), and suites with a ballpark view. One honest trade-off: there is no pool here, so if the kids need to swim between games, the Courtyard or the Holiday Inn below are better picks. Parking is $15 a day in the garage.

Typical summer weekend: ~$200–$250 / night. See it on Expedia →

Courtyard Fort Wayne Downtown — the downtown pool hotel

1150 S Harrison St, Fort Wayne, IN 46802 · 3 min / 0.3 mi to Parkview Field (5-min walk)

Connected to the Grand Wayne Convention Center by skybridge and five minutes on foot from Parkview Field. 249 rooms, a heated indoor pool, Conner’s Kitchen + Bar on site for breakfast and dinner, free rollaway beds and cribs, and kids stay free in their parents’ room. Parking is $15 a day. The pool is the difference-maker here — it is the only downtown hotel on this page with one.

Typical summer weekend: ~$230–$280 / night. See it on Expedia →

Hilton Fort Wayne — best for a whole team

1020 S Calhoun St, Fort Wayne, IN 46802 · 4 min / 0.4 mi to Parkview Field (6-min walk)

246 rooms attached to the Grand Wayne Convention Center, with five meeting rooms and a ballroom that work for a team dinner or a coaches’ meeting. Free airport shuttle, a Starbucks and the Features restaurant on site, and rooms with a sofa bed and microwave. Breakfast is not included — it runs $18–$20 a person — so a family of four should budget for that or eat out. Parking is $15 a night.

Typical summer weekend: ~$190–$240 / night. See it on Expedia →

The Bradley — highest-rated stay downtown

204 W Main St, Fort Wayne, IN 46802 · 4 min / 0.5 mi to Parkview Field (8-min walk)

The boutique pick — and the highest-rated hotel on this page, at 9.6 out of 10 on Expedia. 124 rooms in a 2021 building on Main Street, two restaurants (Arbor and the rooftop Birdie’s), an art gallery in the lobby, and pet-friendly rooms ($50 per stay). Breakfast is cooked to order and costs $3–$20 a person rather than being included, and parking is $19 a day — the priciest on this page — but the rooms and the rooftop are the nicest downtown.

Typical summer weekend: ~$250–$300 / night. See it on Expedia →

Holiday Inn Purdue – Fort Wayne — best pool for the kids

4111 Paul Shaffer Dr, Fort Wayne, IN 46825 · 12 min / 3.2 mi to Parkview Field

The pool hotel. Its heated indoor pool and hot tub were the largest of any hotel in the city when it opened, and it is still the best place on this page for kids to burn off energy between games. Kids 11 and under eat free, parking is free, there is a family suite that sleeps six (king bed plus a twin bunk bed), and the Mastodon Grill serves all three meals. The trade-off is the drive: 12 minutes to downtown, and you will want a car — the hotel sits by the Coliseum and the Purdue Fort Wayne campus, not downtown.

Typical summer weekend: ~$150–$200 / night. See it on Expedia →

Hyatt Place Fort Wayne – Northwest — lowest summer-weekend price

111 W Washington Center Rd, Fort Wayne, IN 46825 · 12 min / 4.5 mi to Parkview Field

The cheapest of the six on a summer weekend, and the easiest on the wallet for a three-night stay: free breakfast buffet, free parking, a seasonal outdoor pool, and rooms that sleep five (two doubles plus a sofa bed). It is up by Glenbrook Square and Spiece Fieldhouse rather than downtown, so the drive is 12 minutes — but the shops and restaurants around Coldwater Crossing are right there, and the Coliseum is six minutes away if your weekend has a second venue.

Typical summer weekend: ~$130–$170 / night. See it on Expedia →

Booking tips for tournament weekends

Book a refundable rate while the schedule is not final. Game times and brackets shift, and a team can be done a day early. A refundable rate costs a little more up front, but it beats paying for a night nobody sleeps in.

Check your tournament’s hotel block first — WBA runs Stay to Play. The World Baseball Academy requires teams in its tournaments to book through the tournament’s housing provider, so a room booked on your own may not count toward your team’s room-night requirement. Other Fort Wayne events run blocks too, and the block rate is often the best one on offer.

The four downtown hotels go first. Hampton Inn, Courtyard, Hilton, and The Bradley are all within a short walk of Parkview Field, and the WBA fields are about five minutes south of downtown by car. If they are full, the two north hotels — Holiday Inn Purdue and Hyatt Place — are 12 minutes away with free parking.

Mind the parking. Downtown parking runs $15 a day at the Hampton, Courtyard, and Hilton, and $19 at The Bradley. The two north hotels include parking free. Add it to the weekend math before you compare nightly prices.

Keep the team together. If several families book the same hotel, ask the front desk about connecting rooms or a group rate. The Hilton and Courtyard both have meeting space that works for a team dinner between games.

Plan the gaps between games. Downtown puts you a walk from Promenade Park, the Fort Wayne Children’s Zoo, and a TinCaps game at Parkview Field itself — worth knowing when there are four hours between pool play and bracket play.
