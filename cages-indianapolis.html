How to pick a cage

Walk-in or membership? Do the twice-a-week math. If your player goes twice a week or more, a membership beats tokens every time — Samp's Hack Shack starts at $30 for unlimited use, and D-BAT and Pro X memberships include unlimited cage reservations. One-off visits belong at The Bat Cave or The Lab.

Reps or coaching? Pick the right building. The Bat Cave, The Lab and both D-BATs are for swings — machine cages, tunnels, show up and hit. Pro X, Feel Good Hitting and Raker's are coaching shops: lessons, evaluations and small groups, booked in advance.

Geography matters in January. North side families: D-BAT Zionsville, Pro X at Grand Park, Feel Good in Carmel. West side: D-BAT Avon and Samp's in Brownsburg. East side: The Bat Cave. South side: The Lab on Meridian and Raker's in Greenwood.

Team blocks: call, don't just show up. D-BAT team cages run from $30 per half hour, but machine cages are first-come, first-served — a 12-player roster showing up unannounced will wait.

Before you go

Confirm hours and prices before you drive over. Winter hours shift around holidays and team blocks, and several of these facilities change their schedule week to week. A two-minute call beats a locked door.

Bring your own bat and helmet. Most machine cages require a helmet in the cage, and house bats are usually the wrong size. Your player's own bat is also the one their swing is built around.

Machine cages are for reps, not lessons. If your player's swing needs fixing, book a lesson at a coaching facility instead of buying more machine rounds — bad reps at 60 mph just groove bad habits faster.
