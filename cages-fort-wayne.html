Batting Cages Near Fort Wayne

Six indoor batting cages and training facilities in the Fort Wayne area — the straightforward walk-in cage on State Boulevard, the nonprofit academy on Freeman Street, and the 13,000-square-foot Diamond on St. Joe Center Road. Northeast Indiana winters are long; these are where Fort Wayne players hit from November to March.

How to pick a cage

Walk-in or academy? Fort Wayne leans academy. Unlike Indianapolis, Fort Wayne has one true walk-in cage — Upper Deck on State Boulevard. Most of the rest are academies with memberships, lessons and team programs, so expect a call or a booking rather than a drop-in.

For a first-timer, start at Upper Deck or WBA. Upper Deck is the lowest-commitment way to get winter swings. World Baseball Academy's small-group training ($225 for a 6-week block, grades 2–7) is the lowest-commitment way to get coached swings.

For a travel team, book a block. The Diamond (nine tunnels), Mad Apple (lanes plus indoor turf) and Strike Zone (team practice area listed at $140 for 90 minutes) are built for team rentals — and The Plex's indoor dome works when the tunnels are booked solid.

One address warning. The Diamond moved — older listings still show its old Keystone Drive address. The current address is 3203 St Joe Center Road. Strike Zone's old Clinton Street location shows as closed; use the State Boulevard address.

Before you go

Confirm hours and prices before you drive over. Winter hours shift around holidays and team blocks, and several of these facilities change their schedule week to week. A two-minute call beats a locked door.

Bring your own bat and helmet. Most machine cages require a helmet in the cage, and house bats are usually the wrong size. Your player's own bat is also the one their swing is built around.

Machine cages are for reps, not lessons. If your player's swing needs fixing, book a lesson at a coaching facility instead of buying more machine rounds — bad reps at 60 mph just groove bad habits faster.
