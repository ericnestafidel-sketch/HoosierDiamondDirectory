Sizing, quickly

Hold the bat out to the side with one hand: if your player can't hold it level for a few seconds, it's too heavy. Most 8U–10U players swing a drop 10; most 11U–12U players a drop 8 or 10. Length runs 26″–29″ for 8U–10U and 29″–31″ for 11U–12U. When in doubt, go lighter — bat speed beats barrel size at every youth age.
