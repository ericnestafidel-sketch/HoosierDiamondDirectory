The winter math

A token cage at $3–$5 a round feels cheap until a 10U player burns eight rounds in 40 minutes. A $50–$80 monthly membership with unlimited or near-unlimited cage time pays for itself by the second visit of the month — and winter is exactly when reps compound. If a facility offers a team rental rate, splitting a two-hour tunnel block across a 12-player roster is usually the cheapest hitting your team will ever get.
