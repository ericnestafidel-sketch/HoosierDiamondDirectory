How to read a tournament listing

Format matters more than the trophy. A 3-game guarantee with pool play into a single-elimination bracket is the standard Indiana weekend. Anything advertising “double elimination” at 10U usually means long gaps between games — bring shade and patience.

Gate fees are the hidden line item. Most hosts charge $5–$15 per adult per day at the gate, on top of the team entry. Budget it before the weekend, not at the fence.

Stay-to-play is real at the big complexes. The largest hosts require out-of-town teams to book through their hotel partners. It's rarely a bad deal, but it does mean the “cheap motel across town” isn't an option.
