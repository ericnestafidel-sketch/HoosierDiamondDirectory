How to pick a 10U tournament

For a first 10U tournament, pick close over big. A 3-game guarantee within an hour's drive beats a 200-team Grand Park weekend for a team still learning tournament baseball — save Grand Park for June, when the team has its feet under it.

Watch the format, not the trophy. Pool play into single elimination is the standard. Anything promising 'double elimination' at 10U usually means long gaps between games — bring shade, snacks, and patience.

Check the bat stamp before you enter. Most of these events take USSSA or USA bats (BPF 1.15 max); a few are USSSA-only. A bat that's legal in rec ball can be illegal on a tournament Saturday — see USSSA vs USA bat rules in plain English.

Before you register

Confirm the division exists. Hosts change age offerings year to year, and a tournament listed as 7U–14U may not run every division every weekend. Check the host page for your exact age before paying.

Fees are per team, gate fees are extra. Entry fees below are as published for 2027 or the typical range for recent seasons where the host has not published a 2027 fee — confirm on the host site before you budget around a number.
