Minor League Baseball in Indiana

Five professional teams play home games in Indiana — three affiliated minor-league clubs (the farm teams of Major League clubs) and two independent pro teams. All five are cheap, family-friendly nights out, and the same hotels tournament families already use. Prices below are the teams' published 2026 prices, checked Nov 28, 2026 — 2027 single-game prices may differ, so check the team's ticket page before you go.

All five teams at a glance

Affiliated means the team is a farm club of a Major League team — the players are working toward the big leagues. Independent means the team signs its own players and is not part of any Major League farm system. The baseball is professional either way; the independent parks are usually the cheapest tickets in the state.

The teams

Ballpark, tickets, and what a family should know before going.

Triple-A

Indianapolis Indians

Victory Field · 501 W Maryland St, Indianapolis, IN 46225 · Pittsburgh Pirates affiliate · International League

The highest level of minor-league ball in the state, in a downtown ballpark in White River State Park — Victory Field opened in 1996 and celebrated its 30th season in 2026. The 2027 season — the club's 125th — starts Friday, March 26 in Toledo, with the home opener Tuesday, April 6 vs. the Iowa Cubs at 6:35 PM. Single-game tickets go on sale in spring 2027.

Tickets (2026 prices): $13 lawn, $16 reserved, $19 box. Kids 14 and under are $1 off; current military get $3 off the gate price. Five Half-Off Wednesday nights ran in 2026 with the code HALF. The Knot Hole Kids Club ($22 for kids 14 and under) includes a ticket to every home game and the chance to run the bases after every Sunday home game.

For families: fireworks nights (including Memorial Day), Sunday Characters visits with PAW Patrol and princesses, a Kids Opening Weekend, and postgame run-the-bases on Sundays. The lawn is the cheapest way in and the best spot for younger kids.

High-A

Fort Wayne TinCaps

Parkview Field · 1301 Ewing St, Fort Wayne, IN 46802 · San Diego Padres affiliate · Midwest League

Downtown Fort Wayne's ballpark and one of the best family nights in the state. The 2027 home opener is Friday, April 2 vs. the Lansing Lugnuts at 7:05 PM, and 2027 individual tickets went on sale November 18, 2026. New for 2027: $10 Ticket Tuesdays and Dollar Dog Nights, with hot dogs for $1.

Tickets: the team states a 2026 range of $9–$21. Kids under 2 get in free on a parent's lap. Parking is $6 per car in the TinCaps lots.

For families: 28 postgame fireworks shows in 2026, including July 4; Family Feast Tuesdays with a value menu; Kids Day character visits (PAW Patrol's Marshall and Peppa Pig in 2026); postgame autographs on Sundays and run-the-bases nights. The Korte Kids Zone has inflatables and a rock wall.

High-A

South Bend Cubs

Four Winds Field at Coveleski Stadium · 501 W South St, South Bend, IN 46601 · Chicago Cubs affiliate · Midwest League

The Chicago Cubs' High-A club in a downtown ballpark that was renovated for 2026 with a new second level of seating. The 2027 home slate starts Tuesday, April 6 vs. the Peoria Chiefs at 7:05 PM, per the team's ticket page. The 2026 season opened Friday, April 3 vs. the Quad Cities River Bandits.

Tickets (2026 prices): $14 field box in advance ($15 on game day), $16 dugout box ($17 on game day), $10 lawn — lawn seats are only sold once the seating bowl sells out.

For families: Fantastic Friday Fireworks after every Friday home game; Sunday FUNday with a pregame player autograph session and catch on the field; a Fun Zone with slides and inflatables past the left-field bleachers and a splash pad down the right-field line; and the Triple Play Tuesday bundle — ticket, jumbo hot dog, and soda for $14 in advance ($15 on game day).

Independent pro

Evansville Otters

Bosse Field · 23 Don Mattingly Way, Evansville, IN 47711 · Frontier League — independent, not an MiLB affiliate · MLB Partner League

The Otters are independent professional baseball — not a farm team of any Major League club — and the oldest current team in the Frontier League, playing in Evansville since 1995. Their home is the draw: Bosse Field opened in 1915 and is the third-oldest ballpark still in regular use for professional baseball in the US, behind only Fenway Park (1912) and Wrigley Field (1914). It was also a filming location for A League of Their Own (1992).

Tickets: 2026 single-game tickets started at $8. For 2027, season plans are on sale now — Grandstand full season $300, 25-game plan $175, 10-ticket flex $70; Lower Level full season $400, 25-game plan $250. The 2026 season opened May 7 vs. the Florence Y'alls; the 2027 single-game schedule had not been published as of Nov 28, 2026.

For families: 14 fireworks shows in 2026, including every Friday home game; Sunday Family Funday with pregame catch on the field; $2 Tuesdays (hot dogs, popcorn, ice cream, water, and 12 oz Busch Light for $2); and Bingo Wednesdays with $2 general admission tickets.

Independent pro

Gary SouthShore RailCats

U.S. Steel Yard · One Stadium Plaza, Gary, IN 46402 · American Association — independent, not an MiLB affiliate

The RailCats are independent professional baseball in the American Association — not a farm team of any Major League club. Their 2027 schedule has been released: the season opens Friday, May 7 at the Chicago Dogs in Rosemont, Illinois, and the home opener is Tuesday, May 11 vs. the Milwaukee Milkmen at U.S. Steel Yard.

Tickets: group tickets and a $50 Fall Pack (four flex passes, a hat, and a water bottle) are on sale now. The team had not published a 2027 single-game ticket price as of Nov 28, 2026 — check the team's site before you go.

For families: the Steel Yard is a short drive from the Indiana Dunes and the Chicago lakefront, so a RailCats game pairs well with a beach day. There is no Gary hotel page on this site yet — if you need a place to stay, look at the Lake County / Michigan City area or the team's site for guidance.

Why this is on a youth baseball site

Families already drive to Indianapolis, Fort Wayne, South Bend, Evansville, and Gary for tournaments — these ballparks are in the same cities, and the same hotels work for a game weekend as for a tournament weekend. A Friday fireworks game is also the easiest way to show a 10-year-old what the next level looks like: the field is bigger, the players are faster, and the game is the same one they play on Saturday morning.

Picking a night: Fridays are the fireworks nights at Fort Wayne, South Bend, and Evansville. Sundays are the family days — run the bases in Indianapolis, catch on the field in South Bend and Evansville. Tuesdays are the value nights — $10 tickets and $1 hot dogs in Fort Wayne, $2 concessions in Evansville, and the Triple Play bundle in South Bend.
